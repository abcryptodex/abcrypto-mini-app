🧭 How to Use Break-Even

3 steps
1

Enter the trade: Enter your Entry MCAP, total capital, and position size before buying.

2

Set your Stop Loss: Use the Stop Loss MCAP shown, while accounting for liquidity and slippage.

3

When price reaches 2R: Move your Stop Loss to Entry to reduce the risk on the trade. Fees may cause your actual break-even level to differ slightly.
